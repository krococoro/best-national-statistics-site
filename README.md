A complete, original national statistics site for Indonesia that runs offline in a
desktop or phone browser from its own static files — no library, no network request,
no build step.

Explore the country by the numbers: headline indicators on the front page (population,
GDP, growth, inflation, poverty, human development), a population explorer with census
history back to 1971 and the 2024 age pyramid, an economy section with sector shares,
inflation and trade, health and education figures, a searchable and sortable table of
the provinces, and an indicator explorer where every series opens with its own chart,
year-by-year table, source and year. Every figure is credited to its source and year.

Open it: `python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory app`, then visit
http://localhost:8080. MIT licensed.
