Nusantara in Numbers is an independent statistics showcase. Figures follow Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) publications — censuses, the National Accounts, Susenas and Sakernas — and the Ministry of Health's SSGI surveys, each credited with its source and year where it appears.

Reference year 2024 unless stated. Values are rounded for display; provincial density is computed from the population and area shown.
